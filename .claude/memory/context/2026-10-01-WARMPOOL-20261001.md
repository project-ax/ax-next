# GKE warm pool feasibility

- The live managed v1beta1 extension CRDs require SandboxClaim.spec.warmPoolRef, expose status.sandbox.name, and allow template networkPolicyManagement=Unmanaged. Read the served CRD rather than assuming older generic examples describe the cluster.
- Isolated gVisor probe on ax-next-std used the deployed image by digest, no credentials or persistent mounts, and deny-all networking. Two no-env claims adopted existing Pod UIDs with zero restarts in 703 ms and 615 ms. A harmless claim.env entry bypassed a ready standby and cold-started in 2,515 ms. Pool replenished; foreground claim deletion removed assigned Pods; temporary namespace confirmed gone. These are claim-to-Pod-ready timings, not AX chat benchmarks.
- Claim environment injection cannot deliver late AX credentials while retaining warm adoption. Proposed design: bounded per-agent/configuration pools to preserve fixed NFS subPaths, plus a runner-core standby wait and authenticated single-use activation. No generic mount of the full Filestore export and no pods/exec grant.
- Proposed activation retrieves a launcher public encryption key and nonce through bounded existing Kubernetes pods/log get, checks stable Pod UID around the read, and sends encrypted host-signed configuration bound to Claim/Sandbox/Pod identities, profile, nonce, and expiry. Host private key stays host-only; preactivation Pods have no session or proxy token. Protocol remains unimplemented and needs negative tests before deployment.
- Design: docs/plans/2026-10-01-agent-sandbox-warm-pools-design.md. Local reproducible cluster probe and sanitized results: /tmp/ax-agent-sandbox-warm-probe.mjs and /tmp/ax-agent-sandbox-warm-probe.log. No production release change during this investigation.
