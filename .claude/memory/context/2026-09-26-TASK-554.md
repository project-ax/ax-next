**Update to TASK-513's "parallelise the reads is pointless" (2026-09-26, TASK-554).**
TASK-513 was right that concurrency alone buys nothing on `workspace-git-server`:
its per-workspace queue runs one op at a time. As of TASK-554 the engine
COALESCES concurrent pinned `workspace:read`s of one (workspace, version) into a
single queued op and a single `git cat-file --batch`. So concurrent pinned reads
ARE now the fast path. `hydrateAgentTier` issues them concurrently (bound 64)
after the first found read names the snapshot. MEASURED full hydrate, loopback,
warm: 2.34 s -> 223 ms at 100 docs, 7.05 s -> 375 ms at 300. What's left is the
two fetches (unpinned list + first read). Unpinned reads still fetch every time
and do not batch. If you add a new bulk reader of the tier, pin its reads and
issue them concurrently. Don't await them one by one.
