# Remote MCP connector form — retained hosts

| Date | Decision | Rationale | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-02 | Display saved and discovered connection hosts separately; keep the optional additional-host field empty when editing and union new entries with existing permissions on save. | The Figma field adds hosts. Reconstructing permissions from that empty field would silently remove saved hosts and break existing connector capabilities. | Prefill the additional-host input with every saved host; obscures the distinction between known hosts and new additions. |
