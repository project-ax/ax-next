Correction (TASK-708, 2026-10-03) to "Reviewer Minor 3" above: "the rare double-send is not
the card's symptom" undersold it. A folded message IS the card's symptom — Working after the
reply for the whole warm window (~4 min, measured live 2026-09-29) — and the stranded id is
sticky, riding every later `chat:start` on that warm runner until `chat:end`. It is reachable
from a second tab, the API, or anything else that enqueues on a busy session. TASK-708 closed
it: the claude-sdk runner now learns of folds from the CLI (`--replay-user-messages`) and
names the folded reqIds on the answering turn's turn-ends (`foldedReqIds`), which
agent-activity drains. See `decisions/2026-10-03-TASK-708.md`.
