| 2026-10-25 | **Close the door: refuse or reset the connection. Never answer 503.** | This client reads 503 as "That part of the app is not running in this deployment" (`HTTP_UNAVAILABLE`, `channel-web/src/lib/http.ts:58,82`). A refused or reset connection is "We could not reach the server just now": `toReadOutcome` and every read hook treat it as transient `failed`, which is true during a restart. Readiness, liveness and the LB health check are unchanged, because `/health` already failed (404) until the last plugin registered it. | An http-server 503 + `Retry-After` gate with listen still in `init()`: kinder to kubectl port-forward, but it collides with the 503 convention above. | 2026-10-25 | **`stopAdmission()` also gates requests: one that arrives while admission is closed has its socket destroyed** before CSRF, routing or any body read. | MEASURED on Node 24.19.0 (scratch probe): after `server.close()`, a keep-alive connection that was busy keeps its socket, and its reply still says `Connection: keep-alive`. Any pipelined request on the same socket is cut too; browsers and undici don't pipeline. | `server.closeAllConnections()` at stop: kills admitted in-flight requests and SSE streams too. A 503 with `Connection: close` for that request: the 503 convention again. |
