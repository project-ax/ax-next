## Refuted / corrected from the brief

- The brief located the `HEAD_SHA` assert in `references/github-project.md`. It is not there: the three sites are `auto-ship/SKILL.md` (merge queue), `yolo-ship/SKILL.md` Phase 6 and Phase 7. Nothing in the fix depends on this, but the card body's pointer is wrong.
- The dispatch brief predicted the `--verify --quiet` → bare `rev-parse` mutant would redden 4 cases (unknown + ambiguous). Measured: **2**. Bare `rev-parse` fails on an ambiguous prefix too, so `--verify` uniquely buys only the 40-hex-string-that-names-nothing case. Recorded in the test header.
