| 2026-11-06 | **Root cause, verified (the builder's reading holds).** The prod routine `rtn_93e6b0aa…` is a Repeat job, not a task: `routine_list`'s `delivery` is non-null only for `kind === 'repeat'` (`packages/routines/src/services.ts:322`, `:328`); "task" in the card is the person's word for any routine.
