| 2026-09-29 | **Rebased the card's premise on main first: it still holds, and the "accidental protection" is the `/` in the placeholder agentId, not the placeholder as such.** Re-ran the probe on `69202ea0` (post TASK-696): 9/9, `begin` still builds its ctx with agentId `'@ax/mcp-oauth'`, and the vault throws `ownerId must match ^[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9_.@-]{0,127}$` at the AGENT-scope step (the `/` fails it), before global scope or the env fallback. | 2026-09-29 | **The allowed shape is `account:<this connector id>:<tag>`, tag REQUIRED (`[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9_-]{0,63}`); the bare `account:<id>` is refused.** Narrower than the card's `account:<id>[:slot]`. | The bare `account:<id>` is where the OAuth callback stores the connector's TOKEN (per user, or per team AGENT at agent scope) and where a workspace connector's company key lives. | 2026-09-29 | **Enforced at `connectors:upsert` (write) and in `mcp-oauth` `begin` (dereference); NOT as a zod refine in `CapabilitiesSchema`.** Deviates from the card's item 1. | `CapabilitiesSchema` also parses every stored row on READ (`validateCapabilities` in `rowToConnector`), and it does not know the connector id.
