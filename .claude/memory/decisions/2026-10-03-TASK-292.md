# Decisions — TASK-292

| Date | Decision | Rationale | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-03 | Cap `tool.pre-call` reject `reason` at 2000 UTF-16 units (`PRE_CALL_REJECT_REASON_MAX`, exported from `@ax/ipc-protocol`) | Same budget as the hold arm's `note`. Both are prose the model reads and the transcript keeps. Longest legit producer today is `@ax/decisions`' `denialSentence` (~300 chars: capability capped at 120, tool name at 64); `GATE_FAILURE_SENTENCE` is ~150. | 500 or 1000 (tighter, but a deny has no reason to get less room than a hold, and a small cap risks cutting a future legit template). |
| 2026-10-03 | Clamp in the host handler (`ipc-core/handlers/tool-pre-call.ts`), not in `@ax/core`'s `reject()` | The handler is the ONLY builder of the wire reject arm, and any subscriber can write any `reason`. `reject()` serves every FireResult consumer across all hooks; clamping there would quietly change non-wire behaviour. This keeps the spirit of the hold precedent: clamp at the producer, before the parse, so a long deny stays that deny instead of becoming a 500 and then a generic gate-failed deny. | Clamp in `reject()` (too broad); schema-only cap (turns long denies into 500s — the exact failure the card names). |
| 2026-10-03 | Truncate without splitting a surrogate pair; no ellipsis | A lone high surrogate is not valid UTF-8 and gets mangled. Otherwise it matches `hold()`'s plain slice. | Append "…" (diverges from the precedent for no product gain). |
| 2026-10-03 | Implemented inline, no per-task subagents | Two-file change, ~20 lines of production code; dispatch overhead would exceed the work. | subagent-driven per yolo-ship default. |
| 2026-10-03 | Test-ordering trap, measured: `ipc-core`'s dispatcher test goes through the BUILT `@ax/ipc-core` (via test-harness/ipc-server), so a src-only edit to a handler is invisible until `pnpm build`. Before the rebuild the new tests failed with 500 "response shape drift" — which incidentally proved they fail against the unfixed handler. | Rebuild before trusting a dispatcher-test result after editing a handler. | — |
