| 2026-10-05 | TASK-876: the `releases both sockets after idle timeout` flake (listener-provider-metering) is a TEST race, not a product race. Fix: `deferIdleTimers()` spies `TLSSocket.prototype.setTimeout`, holds the calls whose ms equals the test's idle value, asserts 2 were held (client side + upstream side), then arms them for real once the upstream holds the request. | The listener arms `clientTls`/`targetTls.setTimeout(idle)` in the same tick it writes the CONNECT 200. With idle=200ms the client's TLS handshake and its request had to land inside 200ms of wall clock. A loop stall over 200ms fires the (correct) idle teardown mid-handshake, so `openTunnel` rejects with `read ECONNRESET`. A 300ms busy-wait after the 200 reproduced it 5/5 with the exact CI signature. Prod idle is 15 min, and tearing down a tunnel that is silent for the whole window is the intended behaviour. After the fix: 50/50 under the 300ms stall, 5/5 under 1000ms. Two mutants (drop one idle arm; no-op idle callback) both redden. | TASK-865's fake `setTimeout`: cannot reach `socket.setTimeout` (measured). A wider window: still a race. Arming idle after the handshake in the product: not a product bug. |
