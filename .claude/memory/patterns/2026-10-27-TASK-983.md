# TASK-983 — check-in inbox and timing

- A durable batch belongs to its dispatch, not to a moving timestamp range. Save a sequence boundary at admission: timestamps alone cannot distinguish overflow waiting at start from a later arrival sharing the same clock timestamp. Retire assigned IDs only when the guarded report commits; report rejection and failed turns leave unseen work available.
- Extending task serialization requires extending executor recovery too. Startup must not interrupt a live check-in owned by another host. Reuse lease renewal, conversation observation and stopped-session fencing; do not free capacity merely because a process-local pending map is empty.
- A reply saved while a check-in is active needs durable wake intent, separate from its text. Return a queued status, keep one active dispatch, and schedule the next check-in when capacity releases. A transient busy error after saving text leaves the person and scheduler with different accounts of what happened.
- Keep full app-update reads bound to the host's current conversation and exact dispatch assignment. The tool supplies only an opaque update ID; the store checks current ownership and the runtime fetches the scoped private blob. Quote previews and full reads as external data, and never grant authority from their content.
