- **Correction to `patterns/2026-09-26-TASK-525.md` ("the notice goes away at the NEXT chat end"):** since TASK-645 the paused state also clears on the next in-conversation incremental pass (idle ~2 min after an assistant turn, or every N user turns) whose provider call resolves — no session end needed. Incremental passes no longer skip a paused user; a walk verifying the clear only has to wait out the idle window, not the reap. A still-paused retry logs `memory_no_credential` at `debug` (only the entry into the pause and `chat:end` log `error`) and fires no `extracting` activity event.
