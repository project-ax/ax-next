## Patterns

- `2026-09-21` — For Docker-backed test startup, use the checked-start wrapper in `packages/test-harness/src/docker-preflight.ts`; its version/info probes have a shared deadline and reject empty or malformed responses before the start is attempted. Passing metadata probes is not proof that container creation or health checks will succeed.

## Anti-Patterns

- `2026-09-21` — The frozen TASK-373 recommendation in the patterns archive to treat a quick docker ps result as a wedge test is superseded: rc=0 with empty output is not evidence of an empty or usable daemon. The later TASK-469 notes distinguish metadata response from actually starting a container; retain that distinction and do not claim a readiness preflight is a full health certificate.
