So `findByRole('textbox')` right after choosing Edit resolves at once to any editor still open on another row, and the test asserts on the wrong input. Scope the wait to the row: `within(row).findByRole(...)` (the `edit()` helper in `packages/channel-web/src/components/workspace/__tests__/TaskSteps.test.tsx`).
