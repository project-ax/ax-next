# mistakes — 2026-09-19 — TASK-428

## "Measured rather than assumed" in a comment is still an unenforced claim

`theme-contrast.test.ts` carried the right numbers for `ApprovalCard`'s surface, in
prose, inside the comment on `QUIET_TEXT_SURFACES` — and concluded "so it passes and gets
no row". Nothing asserted any of it. Two costs landed:

1. A walk re-derived the surface the wrong way and TASK-428 was filed against a defect
   that does not exist. A failing assertion would have been the answer; a paragraph was
   not, because nobody greps comments before filing.
2. The numbers had already drifted. The note said 5.05 / 5.52 / 4.68; the file's own
   formula says 5.03 / 5.51 / 4.66. Small, but it is the difference between a figure that
   is checked every CI run and one that was typed once.

The specific anti-pattern: writing down a measurement *and the reason it needs no test*
in the same breath. If it was worth measuring it is worth a row — and if the pass depends
on something the test cannot see (here, the `/40` alpha), that dependency is the thing to
assert, not to narrate.

Also, on refuting a card: the fact that it does not reproduce is not the end of the work.
The gap that let it be mis-measured is the deliverable.
