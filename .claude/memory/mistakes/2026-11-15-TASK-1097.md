# Mistakes — TASK-1097

| Date | Mistake | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-15 | **A spike's "measured" fact was measured inside a time window and generalised.** TASK-1090 rotated a configuration refresh token and immediately reused the old one, saw it work, and recorded "the old refresh token keeps working". TASK-1093 and TASK-1096 built on that (store once, reuse forever). On real Slack the old token dies a few minutes after its first rotate (TASK-1097 walk, TASK-1129). | When a spike measures that something "still works" after a state change, record WHEN it was checked relative to the change, and re-check after a delay (minutes, and hours) before building on it. "Still valid" is a claim about a duration. |
| 2026-11-15 | **Walk messages posted through the Slack connector are refused as `bot`.** The connector posts carry its `bot_id`; `admission.ts` ignores any event with one. | A Slack walk needs people typing in the Slack client. Don't plan to drive it through the Slack MCP connector. |
